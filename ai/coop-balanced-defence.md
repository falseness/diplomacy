# Defensive-play validation

Run `node ai/test-coop-balanced-defence.js --output-dir artifacts/TASK-109` using the repository-compatible Node runtime. This offline test starts six unmodified generated version-2 games: Tiny H1 seed0 and H4 seed1; Normal H1 seed0 and H2 seed1; Big H1 seed0 and H3 seed1. It runs each through round 40 or a genuine engine terminal result. The flooding threshold remains 40. Multiplayer here means local allied humans; online committed masks have separate integration evidence.

The deterministic human policy attacks any legal target in reach, otherwise defends within three hex edges of an owned town, moving toward nearby enemies and clearing town recruitment output when possible. It buys Noobs and every fourth global order is an Archer, retaining 20 gold and requiring sustainable current income. Every fourth round it attempts a farm, keeping 40 gold afterward, with at most five farm/build queues per town. Congestion can prevent training or farm placement. This is a bounded, deliberately simple defensive policy, not an optimal player. It does not send expeditions to capture mines or neutral towns. Fog is disabled for this sample. Demon combat uses the production SimpleAiPlayer with unrestricted action and default command limits; UI, saving and human-model training side effects are stubbed.

Actual movement and attacks pass through production command validation. Real turn dispatch, spawn selection/placement, unit production, farm building, healing, income, salary, deaths, portal destruction and flooding execute. The test independently reconstructs income from town, suburb, farm and mine counts, checks refresh balances and purchase prices, and asserts minimum training delays from actual orders. It records blocked training output and every submitted command. Initial and completed human unit values are their recruitment prices; cumulative demon value uses the fixed TASK-105 formula applied to installed TASK-108 characteristics. Casualties are reported separately and never subtracted from either cumulative production value.

The suite compares actual spawning with the independent offline selector after filtering the observed living, empty portals. Ordinary local startup does not copy the generated map seed into waveGeneration: the dispatcher starts with wave seed zero. Accordingly actual selection uses zero, while the projected all-portals-available curve retains the map seed used by the historical arithmetic model. Both seeds are recorded; differences are model error, not silently normalized. The authoritative TASK-108 config suite separately validates versioned local/server selection and stats.

Each JSON contains full per-round observations, financial and command events, actual wave availability and spawns, and all per-human typical projected ledgers. A separate final serialized state establishes the actual result and round. Checkpoints are taken at initial state and after a complete human schedule returns to its first surviving human (or immediately on terminal). At these checkpoints the first human's next-turn income/completion has occurred; later humans refresh during their own turns. Event round labels retain that real ordering. A terminal at a human action can repeat the last round number. The projection is synchronous and assumes all humans refresh together, perfect output evacuation, Noob-only purchases, mine acquisition, suburb expansion and eventual neutral-town capture. These are substantive comparison limitations.

A naturally stalled enemy or zero combat in an individual scenario is retained. The overall sample must contain human attacks and each scenario must execute demon AI commands. No terrain, stats, portal HP, winner or round threshold is edited to create outcomes. Portal occupation is counted even when caused by a demon stuck on its own portal; removal can be caused by flooding rather than a human attack. Do not interpret occupancy as intentional human suppression without consulting command and portal records.

Six fixed maps, one defensive policy and round-40 censoring cannot estimate a population win rate or establish the 1.4 target as empirically balanced. Report actual terminal outcomes separately from ongoing-at-40 games. Read the deviations, recruitment congestion, losses and environmental results alongside the arithmetic reports. All outputs are local artifacts; no deployment is involved.
