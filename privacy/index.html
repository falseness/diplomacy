Diplomacy — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive a signed token from Google that contains your Google account identifier, email address and name. We use it only to recognize you as the same player across sessions. We store only an anonymous identifier derived from your Google account ID. We do not store your email address, name or profile picture.

Game data

We store the games you play (moves, game state and game settings), linked to that anonymous identifier, so that online games can continue between sessions.

How we use it

This data is used only to run the game. We do not sell or share it with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or analytics.

Deleting your data

To delete your data, email us at the address below and we will remove your games and identifier.
